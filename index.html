To My Darling

I built this warm corner of the world just for you so whenever the noise of everything gets too loud you can step inside and rest your head

You carry so much with such grace even on the days when school sucks the soul right out of you I see how hard you work and I see the pure sweetness of your heart

My only wish in this life is to bring that light right back into your eyes to make you smile when you are tired to remind you that you never have to walk through any of it alone

You are my quiet space and my favorite thought and I love you more deeply than any words on paper could ever hold

this is for you my darling

Whenever you feel like the world is falling apart, remember you can always come back here and feel my love right next to you.

ALWAYS & FOREVER YOURS
